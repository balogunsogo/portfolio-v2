Context

Profiles

Digital employee profiles.

This application gives staff a digital business card. I worked on three parts: the public card a recipient sees after scanning a QR code, the staff dashboard for generating and sharing QR codes and links, and the admin portal for managing access, activating and deactivating profiles, and reporting.

Integration

Connecting a modern platform to a legacy core.

In 2026 I started on a shared .NET service that lets a modern insurance platform book policies in an older Oracle-based core system.

I built the first version of the booking API: request contracts for each product, validation with readable error messages, and adapters that translate each request into what the core expects. That means product codes, reference IDs from a shared lookup layer, and lists flattened into fixed positions. I also worked on duplicate-request protection and on separating invalid requests from system failures.

Bookings are processed asynchronously. I wrote the first version of the background worker that picks up queued bookings in small batches, passes each one to the right product handler and saves the outcome. A bad record fails on its own without stopping the batch, and both the original and translated requests are kept for tracing.

Claims

Claims settlement and turnaround time.

On the claims application, I added settlement to more than one beneficiary. The change went through every layer: the claim breakdown screen, browser and server validation, the domain model and ORM mappings, the database and the payment files.

Allocations must add up to the settlement exactly, so rounding leftovers are handled explicitly and every total reconciles. The same account can’t be paid twice, concurrent edits can’t overwrite each other, removed beneficiaries stay on record and paid allocations are locked. Payments go through the existing ledger, with a reference for each beneficiary’s instruction.

I also investigated a production error in the claims flow and traced the root cause to an existing lookup that didn’t handle a missing value.

On the same application, I built an event-driven turnaround clock. It starts when the required documents are complete, pauses while a valid missing-document request is open and stops at the final decision. Alongside it, I added the claim’s message history, duplicate checks on uploads and file-signature validation.
